# Fox Crossing Flipbook

A registered aerial image time series for Humboldt County parcel APN 314-213-030 (37 acres) and the 1,516-acre unnamed drainage it sits in, which meets the Mad River at its northeast corner.

**View it:** https://dylan-keel.github.io/fox-crossing-flipbook/

## What is in it

- Twenty dates from March 1942 to October 2025
- Four views: the drainage, the parcel, a 3D view of the parcel looking northwest, and a 3D view of the whole drainage
- Optional overlays: parcel boundary, drainage divide, streams, and CAL FIRE historical fire perimeters
- Play, pause, zoom, and save any frame as a still

## Sources

- 1942 to 1970: scanned prints from the Humboldt County Aerial Photograph Collection, Cal Poly Humboldt
- 1993: USGS National Aerial Photography Program (UCSB Library scans)
- 2005 to 2024: USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program
- 2025: Esri World Imagery
- Parcel boundary: Humboldt County GIS. Streams: National Hydrography Dataset. Elevation: USGS 3DEP 10 m. Fire perimeters: CAL FIRE

## Caveats

Historic frames were orthorectified for this project and sit within a few metres of each other, more on steep ground. The parcel boundary, drainage divide and stream lines are not a survey.

## Files

`index.html` is the viewer. The `.jpg` files are the frames for each view and date.
